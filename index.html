How To Play

How To Play

Hit the mapped key when a falling note touches the hit line. Timing tiers are Perfect, Good, and Ok.

Righty layout: K hi-hat, H/J snare, Space bass, I/O low tom, Y/U high tom, 9/0 ride, 7/8 crash.

Lefty layout: D hi-hat, G/H snare, Space bass, E/R low tom, T/Y high tom, 3/4 ride, 5/6 crash.

Use Jam Mode to freestyle with no song, and Start Recording to capture your hits and replay them as a custom chart.
